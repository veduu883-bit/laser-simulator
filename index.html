Stimulated emission and photon amplification

The gain medium uses the populations above: each lane holds 8 atoms, and the share in E2 is N2/(N1+N2). A photon meeting an excited atom (filled) may stimulate a copy of itself; one meeting a ground-state atom (hollow) may be absorbed. This is a qualitative demonstration.

The model behind the numbers

In steady state (ignoring stimulated processes), N3 = (W/S)·N1 and N2 = (W/A)·N1, with N1+N2+N3 = N. Hence N2 > N1 exactly when W > A: pumping must outpace the spontaneous decay of the upper laser level. A large S keeps E3 almost empty, so nearly every pumped atom reaches E2.
